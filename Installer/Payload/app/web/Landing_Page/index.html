Game Project

What is Pixel Chaser?

Pixel Chaser is an arcade-style 2D chase game built from the ground up in Java Swing by the NovaBit Studios team. You control a nimble pixel character on a neon grid world, racing to collect golden coins before a relentless enemy closes in.

The twist: every coin you collect speeds up your pursuer. The challenge escalates fast — manage your stamina bar, time your dash, and outmanoeuvre the enemy to climb the high-score table.

Built as a passion project with zero external dependencies, Pixel Chaser ships with its own runtime. Download, double-click, play.

Tester Program

Become a Beta Tester

We're looking for dedicated players to help us polish Pixel Chaser before the public release. Testers get early access, direct contact with the dev team, and their names in the credits.

▶

What you'll do

  • Play a minimum of one full session per week
  • Submit bug reports via the feedback form
  • Note any performance issues or visual glitches
  • Test all three language modes (VI / EN / ZH)
  • Verify scoring and high-score persistence
★

What you'll get

  • Early access to all beta builds
  • Direct feedback channel with developers
  • Name in the game credits
  • Exclusive "Founding Tester" badge
  • Priority access to future NovaBit titles
▼

How to join

  • Download and install Pixel Chaser below
  • Launch the game and complete one session
  • Follow the in-game prompt after closing
  • Submit your session report via the portal
  • Wait for confirmation email from the team
Limited spots available for this beta cycle. Invitation emails are sent in batches.
